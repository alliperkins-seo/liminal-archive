Rebuilt from your photographs: the red brick, the white pediment, the word Fifteen over the door. The cul-de-sac keeps its shape; the doors do not keep their contents. Behind each one is a record from the archive — and when you close one, the doors quietly trade dreams, drifting toward whatever you last read. Nothing here maps cleanly. That's the point.
