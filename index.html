The Liminal ArchiveBrandywine Walk

A game built from dreams and memories, from journals going back decades, and from one girl’s curiosity about what it would be like to walk around in them awake. Some you read. Some you walk into. The houses keep adding rooms when nobody is watching. The animals already know you’re here. Behind each door, a fragment, and the doors trade while your back is turned. Wander, collect, play, ask questions. The rules change when the dreams do. A girl grew up in here, more or less. She keeps coming back to the same places. So will you.
